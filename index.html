Privacy Policy

App: Brain Training (Android package com.braintraining.brain_training)
Developer: Vishvesh Busa
Contact: vishvesh.busa.164@gmail.com
Effective date: 6 November 2026

Brain Training offers short memory, mathematics, reaction and logic games with levels, statistics, achievements and daily goals. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. In short: your game progress stays on your phone; an account is optional; and ads are shown by Google.

1. Information on your phone

The app saves these on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Your finished games: the training, level, score, correct and incorrect answers, time taken and date.
  • Your level progress, achievements, streaks, daily goals and daily plan.
  • Your settings: theme, reminder choices, the categories and daily time you chose for your plan.

This information is not uploaded by the app and there is no cloud backup. It stays on your phone until you clear it (Settings → Data → Clear all progress), clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

Moving your progress yourself. Settings → Export progress copies your progress as text to your phone's clipboard, and Import progress adds text you paste back. What you do with the copied text (for example sending it in a message to yourself) is your choice. Statistics → Save as PDF hands a report to Android's print and save dialog; the app does not send it anywhere.

2. Reminders

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules notifications on your phone and asks Android for permission to show them. They are created and shown locally. The app does not use a push notification service and the reminder text contains no personal information.

3. Optional account

You can use the whole app as a guest. If you choose to create an account, we handle:

  • your email address and password, which are processed by Firebase Authentication (a Google service). The app never stores your password;
  • your display name, if you set one;
  • an internal account ID and the date the account was created, and whether you verified your email.

These are used only to create and secure your account, to sign you in and to send password reset and verification emails. An account does not back up your game progress; that stays on your phone. You can delete your account at any time: Profile → Delete account, or see section 7.

4. Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob . AdMob and Google may collect and use information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system, how you interact with ads and approximate location derived from your IP address, to show and measure ads, to personalise them where you allow it, and to prevent fraud. The app does not pass your game results to advertisers.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app shows Google's consent message before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice later in Settings → Privacy. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings. More about how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. What we do not collect

The app has no analytics or crash-reporting service. It does not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, files or precise location, and it does not ask for those permissions. The app has no in-app purchases and no payment features, so we collect no payment information of any kind. If purchases are added in a future version, we will update this policy before they are available. We do not sell your personal information.

6. Who receives information

Only the Google services that make the app work: Firebase Authentication (accounts) and AdMob (ads), and Google Play, which delivers the app. Google's own privacy policy applies to what it handles: policies.google.com/privacy. Google may process data in countries other than yours. We do not share your game data with anyone else.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your game data: Settings → Data → Clear all progress, or uninstall the app. Use Export progress first if you may want it back.
  • Delete your account (if you made one): Profile → Delete account in the app, or email vishvesh.busa.164@gmail.com from the address of the account with the subject “Delete my Brain Training account” and we will delete it within 30 days and confirm.
  • Ads: change your consent in Settings → Privacy where it is offered, and manage your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Reminders: turn them off in Settings, or in Android's app notification settings.
  • Your rights: depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act or California law) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. The only personal data we can look up is the account email and name above. Contact us using the email at the top.

8. Security

Information between the app and Google services travels over encrypted connections, and passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication. Your game progress is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. No system is perfectly secure.

9. Children

Brain Training is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information (for example an email address), contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. If a change matters to you (for example a new feature that uses your data) we will say so in the app or on this page.
